Modelling in short

Verifiable records have brv:verificationStatus and a checked source with access date; use verified-primary and verified-secondary for question answering. schema:sameAs links only identifiers, authority records and profile pages of the same entity. Broken or unsafe URLs are kept as strings (brv:deprecatedUrl), never as links. Descriptions of citing documents are paraphrases.
